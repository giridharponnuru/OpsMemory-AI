🚀 We built OpsMemory AI at HackWithHyderabad 3.0!

Production incidents repeat, but engineering teams often have to rediscover the same root causes and fixes.

Our solution is an AI Incident Response Agent powered by Hindsight that creates a persistent organizational memory:

🚨 Incident → 🧠 Recall past incidents → 🤖 Reason → ✅ Resolve → 💾 Retain the lesson → 🔁 Improve the next response

Unlike a stateless chatbot, the agent gets better as the incident history grows.

The core of our project uses Hindsight's Retain, Recall and Reflect capabilities to turn incident postmortems into reusable operational experience.

We focused on one question:

“What if your incident-response agent actually remembered what your team learned?”

Built with Python, Streamlit and Hindsight.
